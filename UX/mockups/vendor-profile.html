About

Studio Saanjh is the work of Priya and Arjun Mehra, a husband-and-wife team based in Fremont. We've photographed over 80 South Asian weddings and family events across California — from intimate Punjabi sangeets in Berkeley backyards to 500-guest Gujarati weddings at the Hyatt Santa Clara.

Our approach is documentary first: we'd rather catch your dadi laughing during the ardaas than line everyone up for the same staged shot you've seen on Instagram a thousand times. The result is an album that feels like your family, not a stock catalog.

What's included

  • Pre-event consultation and shot-list planning
  • Two photographers for full event coverage (8–12 hours typical)
  • 500+ professionally edited photos with online gallery
  • Download and print rights for all images
  • Optional cinematic highlight reel (3–5 minutes)
  • Backup gear and second-shooter coverage on multi-day events
